About

I'll never forget being on the playground in 5th grade, so annoyed that my friend wouldn't stop talking about Minecraft, but like every kid, my free time soon became Minecraft time. I was always the one hosting servers, tinkering with configs, etc.. Till eventually I wanted to write my own code, my own Bukkit plugins, on a server I spent most of my childhood on. It was some truly terrible Java code, but I got to test in production with 300-500 concurrent players at 16. Something that I still can't believe I was allowed to do.

From there, I got into Linux, and not just any Linux. I was influenced by all the “cool kids,” and after 6 tries while in high school, I was officially able to say, “I use arch btw.” I was in love with never having to remove Edge or OneDrive, or decline to pay for Office 365, true peace. With a decade now on Linux, I can't imagine developing on anything else.

At the University of Michigan, I got to do some absolutely amazing things, like designing processors, understanding microarchitecture, robotics, and novel sensor designs. It's hard to ever pick one thing after that, but I still love programming by hand and digging into new languages and architects.

R10K Out-of-Order Processor

Computer Architecture capstone · team of 6 · Fall 2024

A 2-way superscalar out-of-order CPU in SystemVerilog. I wrote the first reorder buffer, designed the load/store units, and worked on testing and optimization. The final design ran at 1.34 weighted CPI at a 9.5 ns clock. Speculative loads took CPI from 1.90 to 1.31.
